I spent eight years as CMO at C85 GmbH and worked hands-on in UX, design systems, and product testing. I also built an e-commerce brand on Amazon in Germany and the UK, taking products from idea to launch and handling PPC, forecasting, and marketing along the way.

PPC was my favorite part. I overtook the top two competitors in my niche in eight weeks. The ad bill was memorable, too. That experience is part of why I’m building Nordstern42 with Tobi: to help Amazon sellers understand where their money goes and what’s left after costs.

## Focus

- [**Nordstern42**](https://nordstern42.de/) — A financial toolkit for Amazon sellers, built in public.
  - **Margin Calculator** — Estimate what’s left after costs when selling in Germany, across the EU, or in the UK.
  - **Rate Card** — Work out the most you can pay per unit and still hit your target margin.
  - **Discount Check** — See how your discounts performed before deciding which offers to run again.
- [**Amazon Seller Prompt Library**](https://github.com/itsChrizzpee/amazon-seller-prompt-library) — 70 German-language AI prompts for everyday seller tasks, from sourcing and listings to PPC and keyword tracking.

## Where I create leverage

- **Amazon Operations & PPC** — I take products from idea to launch, manage ads, forecast demand, and make videos to market the brand. I’m building a reporting workflow to see ad spend and performance across Germany and the UK in one place. Two marketplaces are plenty. I don’t need twice the tabs.
- **UX & Design Systems** — I improve user flows and work with component libraries to make products easier to use and faster to build. At Boxine, I extended component libraries in Adobe XD, Figma, and Storybook.
- **Manual & Automated Testing** — I’ve done hands-on QA alongside UX work for products including Zazoon, Viamor, and Diligram. At Zazoon, I worked on a Claude-assisted workflow that ran browser checks from Markdown test plans and produced test logs and Playwright specs.
- **AI & Finance Workflows** — Month-end close felt like stubbing my toe on the same bed frame every month. So I built a semi-automated workflow to file invoices, track what I’ve uploaded to DATEV, and spot duplicates or missing documents. Now I have an archive I can actually find things in.
